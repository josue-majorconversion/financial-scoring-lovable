# Financial Scoring

Build a modern, mobile-first landing page for FinancialScoring targeting insurance agents who scan a QR code at a live event. The page should feel like a high-end AI-powered product experience, not a traditional lead vendor page.

Goal

Convert insurance agents into subscribers by showing them that their existing leads are worth more than they think, and that financial scoring helps them make more money with fewer calls.

Brand

Company name: FinancialScoring

Tagline: We don't sell leads, we replace them.

Primary colors: Rich black background, mint green accents, white text

Feel: Bold, modern, dark, clean, high-contrast, AI-powered

Logo element: A magnifying glass icon with a score number inside it

Tone

Provocative but not offensive

Curious and intriguing

Simple and direct

High value

Focused on more money, fewer calls, and better leads

Page sections

Section 1 — Hero

Goal: Stop agents immediately and create curiosity.

Headline: Your leads are more valuable than you know

Subheadline: Financial scoring helps agents uncover more deals, higher commissions, and fewer calls — from the leads you already have.

Design notes:

Full screen dark hero

Mint green accent on headline or key words

Large bold typography

Include a magnifying glass icon with a score number inside it

Add a subtle animated or glowing background effect to feel premium and AI-powered

CTA buttons:

Primary: Experience Scoring

Secondary: See How It Works

Section 2 — Proof statement

Goal: Show agents they are sitting on untapped money.

Headline: It is estimated that $1 million in unpaid commissions sits in the leads you have already called.

Subheadline: Score every lead. Make more money.

Design notes:

Bold stat displayed large

Mint green highlight on the dollar amount

Keep it short and punchy

No more than 2-3 lines of copy

Section 3 — Simple comparison

Goal: Show the difference between traditional leads and scored leads.

Display a side by side comparison card:

Left side — Traditional leads:

Average commission: $1,200

Chargeback rate: 30%

More calls required

Lower persistency

Right side — Scored leads:

Average commission: $8,800

Chargeback rate: 8%

Fewer calls required

Higher persistency

Design notes:

Use a card or table style layout

Left side should feel dull or faded

Right side should feel bright, mint green, energized

Add "Do the math" as a bold callout underneath

Section 4 — Rotating scoring examples

Goal: Show how scoring actually works with real client examples.

Design notes:

Build a rotating or auto-scrolling carousel

Each card shows:

A fake client name and score (example: Sarah — Score 74)

A score breakdown bar showing categories like cash flow, tax efficiency, insurance, retirement

An AI copilot recommendation panel next to the score that says what products this client needs and why

An estimated commission value at the bottom

Include at least 3 examples rotating through:

A final expense example

An IUL example

An annuity or 401k rollover example

The AI copilot panel should feel like a chat or AI assistant response

Label it clearly as an AI-powered recommendation

Mobile friendly — stack vertically on mobile

Headline above this section: Ask the right questions. Let AI do the rest.

Subheadline: Financial scoring asks a few simple questions and your built-in AI copilot whispers exactly what your client needs and why.

Section 5 — Why agents want this

Goal: Speak directly to agent pain points and dream outcomes.

Headline: Less work. More money.

Display 4-6 benefit blocks with icons:

Make fewer calls

Make more deals

Earn higher commissions

Find money in leads you already called

Improve persistency

Serve your clients better

Design notes:

Clean icon grid

Short 2-4 word labels

Mint green icons on dark background

Section 6 — The movement

Goal: Create FOMO and urgency around becoming a scoring analyst.

Headline: Your clients are being replaced by certified scoring analysts every single day.

Subheadline: Financial scoring bridges the gap between insurance agent and financial professional. You are either becoming one or losing clients to one.

Add a badge or certification visual:

Show a "Certified Scoring Analyst" badge or emblem

Make it look credible and desirable

Subtext below badge:

Clients want this. They want to be scored. They want a bigger picture. They want someone who asks the right questions.

CTA button:

Become a Certified Scoring Analyst

Section 7 — Social proof

Goal: Reduce skepticism and build credibility.

Headline: What agents are saying

Add 3 testimonial cards:

Use placeholder names and quotes for now

Quotes should reflect themes like:

Making more money from old leads

Feeling more confident in conversations

Getting bigger commissions

Fewer chargebacks

Design notes:

Dark card style

Mint green accent on name or quote marks

Keep quotes short and punchy

Section 8 — Final CTA

Goal: Convert the agent into a subscriber or capture their information.

Headline: Scored clients are awaiting your call.

Subheadline: Become a subscriber today and start uncovering the commissions already sitting in your leads.

Primary CTA button: Become a Subscriber

Secondary option:

Add a simple email capture form below the button

Label it: Not ready yet? Enter your info and we will reach out.

Include a note: One lucky subscriber wins a full annual subscription. Enter to win.

Design notes:

Full width dark section

Mint green button

Keep it clean and simple

End strong

Overall design rules

Dark rich black background throughout

Mint green as the primary accent color

White body text

Bold modern sans-serif typography

Mobile-first layout

Minimal copy — short phrases over paragraphs

Heavy use of visuals, cards, and score elements

AI copilot panels should feel like a real AI assistant

Score visuals should include a number, a bar, and category breakdowns

Page should feel like a premium product demo

Smooth scroll experience

Subtle animations or glows to feel modern and cutting edge

Scored clients are awaiting your call.
